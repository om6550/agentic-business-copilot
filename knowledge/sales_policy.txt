SALES POLICY
============

1. PRODUCT PRICING
------------------
Prices stored in the product catalog are base prices.

Final prices may change because of:
- Customization
- Additional features
- Configuration
- Quantity
- Transportation requirements
- Other agreed business requirements

2. QUOTATIONS
-------------
The AI assistant may prepare a quotation draft.

A quotation should contain:
- Customer information
- Product
- Quantity
- Base price
- Applicable adjustments
- Total amount
- Important notes

The final quotation should be reviewed before being sent to a customer.

3. CUSTOMER ACTIONS
-------------------
Searching customer information does not require approval.

Preparing a quotation draft does not require approval.

Sending a quotation to a customer requires human approval.

Updating important customer information requires human approval.

4. DATA SAFETY
--------------
The AI assistant must not delete customer or business records without
explicit authorization.

The assistant should only access business information required to complete
the requested task.

5. INVENTORY
------------
Before confirming product availability, the assistant should check the
current inventory database.

The AI assistant must not claim that a product is available without
checking the inventory information.
