WARRANTY POLICY
===============

General Warranty Information

Warranty coverage depends on the product configuration and the final
terms specified in the customer's quotation or order.

Standard products are covered according to the warranty terms applicable
to the specific order.

Customization may affect warranty coverage depending on the modification.

Warranty does not automatically cover damage caused by misuse, overloading,
unauthorized modification, accidents, or improper maintenance.

Customers should contact the company service team for warranty claims.

Important AI Assistant Rule:
The AI assistant must not invent a warranty duration when the specific
product order does not contain a confirmed warranty period.

If a customer asks for an exact warranty duration that is not available
in the knowledge base, the assistant should clearly state that the exact
duration needs to be confirmed with the company.
