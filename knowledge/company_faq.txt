COMPANY FAQ
===========

Q: What products does the company manufacture?
A: The company manufactures Standard Tractor Trailers, Hydraulic Trailers,
Heavy Duty Trailers, Agriculture Trailers, and Custom Trailers.

Q: Can customers request customization?
A: Yes. Customers can request customized trailer dimensions, configurations,
and other requirements depending on feasibility.

Q: How can a customer request a quotation?
A: A customer can contact the sales team and provide the required product,
quantity, and customization requirements.

Q: Are the listed product prices final?
A: No. Product prices are base prices. The final quotation may change based
on customization, additional features, configuration, and business requirements.

Q: Which trailer is suitable for heavy loads?
A: The Heavy Duty Trailer has a listed capacity of 12 tons and is designed
for high-load agricultural and transport applications.

Q: Which trailer has hydraulic unloading?
A: The Hydraulic Trailer is designed with a hydraulic tipping mechanism
for efficient unloading.

Q: Can the AI assistant create a final quotation automatically?
A: The AI assistant can prepare a quotation draft. Final business actions
that require approval must go through the human approval process.
